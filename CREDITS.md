# Asset credits

The artwork is by **Kenney**, from [Pixel Platformer](https://kenney.nl/assets/pixel-platformer). The terrain tiles are 18 × 18 pixels. The character sheet is sliced into 24 × 24 pixel frames. The original license is included at `Assets/Kenney-Pixel-Platformer-License.txt`.

The setup guide also stages these Kenney packs outside the project for future lessons:

- [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds)
- [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds)
- [Music Jingles](https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles)

All four packs are released under **CC0**. Each pristine local pack retains its original `License.txt`. The asset sources were downloaded from Kenney's official website on 13 October 2026.

The movement controller and room configuration follow Lucian Vîlcea's ASE Multimedia [week 2 exercises](https://docs.lucianvilcea.ro/multimedia/exercise-5-a-walker-that-walks).
